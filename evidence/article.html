Elm Street bridge reopens after two-year repair

The Elm Street bridge, built in 1932, reopened to traffic on Monday after a two-year repair that replaced its deck and both approach ramps.

The repair cost 4.2 million dollars and was paid for by the county roads fund. The bridge carries about 6,000 vehicles a day across the Mill River.

Cyclists get a protected lane on the north side for the first time.
